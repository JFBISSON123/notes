How this works

Title and note are encrypted on this device with AES-256-GCM. The key is stretched with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 rounds) and a fresh random salt. Only the scrambled text leaves the app, so email, text, Garmin, WhatsApp or a file only ever carry ciphertext. The check code comes from the key through the same slow stretching and does not reveal it. Capitals, extra spaces and dashes in a key do not matter, and key words can be shortened to their first four letters. Nothing is saved on the device and the app makes no network requests.
