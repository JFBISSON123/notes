Notes

Both people must enter the same key. Agree on it in person, never send it with a note. The key is kept only while this screen is open and is wiped when you lock or leave.

How this works

Title and note are encrypted on this device with AES-256-GCM. The key is stretched with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 rounds) and a fresh random salt for every note. Only the scrambled text leaves the app, so email, text, Garmin, WhatsApp or a file only ever carry ciphertext. Nothing is saved on the device and the app makes no network requests.
